# Mission 14: AI as Analyst

**Phase:** D  
**Core skill:** AI-assisted investigation  

## Situation
An AI analyst has produced a confident assessment containing both correct and incorrect conclusions.

## Objective
Review every material claim, accept or reject it with evidence, and document at least two incorrect/incomplete AI conclusions.

## AI context
This mission makes AI fallibility explicit. The learner must use evidence to challenge the model.

## Rules of engagement
- Work only with the local training files in this mission.
- AI assistance is allowed and expected where useful.
- AI output is not evidence.
- Preserve objective verification evidence.
- Complete `ai_work_record.json`.

## Deliverables
- analysis_review.json classifies claims
- at least two rejected claims
- each decision cites evidence
- completed ai_work_record.json

## Finish
Run `range verify 14 <workspace>`. Technical completion is determined by the verifier, not by AI confidence.
