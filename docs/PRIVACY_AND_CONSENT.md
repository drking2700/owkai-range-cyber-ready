# Privacy and Consent Baseline

- Collect the minimum learner identity needed to operate the cohort.
- Veteran self-identification is optional and must not be required to participate.
- Learner workspaces/repositories are private by default for pilots.
- Portal stores mission status, AAR score, review state, and cost metadata; it does not require storage of full AI-assistant transcripts.
- AI Work Records should summarize material AI assistance rather than copy full private conversations by default.
- External model providers may not be enabled for learner PII until retention/use terms are reviewed and accepted.
- Never include production/customer data in Range prompts, repositories, or mission assets.
