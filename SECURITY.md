# Security Boundary

Operation: Cyber Ready is a local training environment.

- Do not place production/customer data in mission workspaces.
- Do not add production credentials to `.env`, examples, tests, or mission fixtures.
- AI assistance has no tool-execution capability in V1.
- External AI providers are opt-in and require separate retention/privacy review.
- Mission vulnerabilities are synthetic and scoped to local files/configurations.
- Report accidental secret inclusion or cross-learner data exposure before continuing a cohort.
